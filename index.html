Privacy Policy — ExactTake

Last updated: November 26, 2026

ExactTake is a video capture app. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

No data is collected or transmitted

ExactTake contains no tracking code, no third-party advertising or analytics SDK, and makes no network requests. Everything the app does stays strictly on your device.

Camera and microphone

Access to the camera and microphone is used only to film the videos you record yourself. Nothing is recorded or transmitted without your explicit action (pressing the record button).

Photo library

Videos are saved directly to your local photo library (Photos). ExactTake can also read back an already saved clip, only for the "+Xs" feature (appending what you kept filming after stopping), and add a caption to it with "Rename", both started explicitly by you. These operations never leave your device.

Location (optional)

If you enable geotagging in Settings, your position is saved in the video file's own metadata, the same way any native photo or video app would. This information stays inside the file, on your device and in whatever backups you control (iCloud, if you use it). ExactTake never sends it to any third party.

Settings

Your preferences (recording durations, microphone choice, video format, etc.) are stored locally on your device (UserDefaults) and are never shared.
