How does ExactTake film what happened before I tapped record?

While the camera is open, ExactTake keeps the last seconds in memory, without saving anything. When you tap one of the record buttons, those seconds are put at the start of your clip. Nothing is written to your phone until you actually record.

What do the three record buttons do?

Each one keeps a different amount of time from before your tap (for example 10 seconds, nothing, or 30 seconds). You can change the durations in Settings, up to 2 minutes.

I stopped too soon. Can I get the rest?

Yes. Right after you stop, a "+Xs" button appears: tap it and what you kept filming is added to the same clip, as long as it is still in memory.

Where are my videos?

In your Photos library, like any video filmed with the iPhone camera. Tap the small thumbnail at the bottom left to open Photos. "Rename" adds the name you type as the video's caption in Photos, so you can search for it.

My external microphone is not used

Plug it in, then check the microphone name at the top of the screen. You can pick it manually in Settings, and choose which channel to keep for two-transmitter receivers such as the DJI Mic.

Privacy
